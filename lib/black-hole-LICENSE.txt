Black hole renderer reference and attribution

The native WebGL2 renderer in themes/next/source/js/black-hole.js adapts
physically inspired ray-bending, Keplerian differential rotation, cyclic
noise advection and Doppler-beaming concepts from:
https://github.com/dgreenheck/webgpu-black-hole

Its finite-thickness volume integration, 3D noise texture, GLSL rendering
passes, composition and browser lifecycle are implemented locally. The
renderer is an artistic approximation, not an exact general-relativity
simulation. The upstream license is reproduced below.
